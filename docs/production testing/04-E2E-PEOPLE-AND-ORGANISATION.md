## 3a. One gateway for every new employee (#694, #692, #690)

Since #694 every way of creating an employee (the Add employee form, the import, a device
migration, SCIM and SSO just-in-time) goes through one gateway that looks for the person first and
**asks before it creates a second record**. The refusal is
`App\Exceptions\EmployeeAlreadyExists`; the form shows it through
`src/src/features/employees/components/existing-employee-dialog.tsx`, and the outcomes are
`matched`, `needs_review`, `archived` and `former_employee`
(`src/src/features/employees/likeness.ts:12`). #692 made a **contradicted** match go to review
instead of merging, and stopped an archived employee being added a second time.

The refusal is an RFC-7807 **409** of one of two kinds, and the kind decides what the dialog
offers:

- **`employee-exists`**: a current employee on an identifier strong enough to act on (the same
  national ID, email, or phone and name). **Not overruled**: the dialog offers "Close" and
  "Open their record", and there is no "Add as a new person".
- **`employee-needs-confirmation`**: someone who *may* be this person, by a weaker match, a tie, a
  match another detail contradicts, a leaver, or a deleted record. The dialog offers "Cancel"
  and "Add as a new person", which sends the same form again with `confirm_new: true`.

Either way the body carries `outcome` and up to **five** `candidates`, each with its name and
the reasons it matched, which the dialog shows as badges.
**Live since v2026.11.12.1 (`77e3cfe1a9` and `039ec9accf` in `c8cc3a4b2d`), confirmed with
`release-status.sh` on 2026-11-12.**

## 4a. One read for every list of people (#696, #699, #700, #701, #695, #693)

Since #696 there is **one** reader for every list of people (the wizard's People step,
Employees → Import and a device migration), and the import **is** the staged review: the list is
read, each line matched against ETHR, and nothing is written until it is committed.
`POST /migration/read` reads a pasted grid or an uploaded workbook; the commit is
`POST /migration/batches/{batch}/commit`, throttled to **30 a minute**
(`api/app/Providers/AppServiceProvider.php:419-420`), and it adds the list **in slices**, so a long
list survives the host's 30-second limit. A list holds at most `EmployeeImportGrid::MAX_PEOPLE`
**2,000** people; the browser's workbook reader stops at `MAX_LINES` **5,000** lines
(`src/src/features/onboarding/v2/xlsx-grid.ts:25`). A staged list is hard-deleted after
`MigrationBatch::RETENTION_DAYS` **30** days with no activity on the batch **or any of its rows**
(#693). Section 4's `IMP-E-*` cases still cover the CSV **endpoints**, which no screen calls any more,
and the export; these cover the read, the review and the saved column names.

**Live since v2026.11.12.1 (`4658f99ca3`, `3791d65bb3`, `1bba5b5e41`, `3a693dd4fd` and
`c8cc3a4b2d` itself), confirmed with `release-status.sh` on 2026-11-12.**

A file is never uploaded on this path: the browser reads it and sends the cells as text, so the
host's 2 MB limit (K-16) does not apply, but a **5 MB** check in the browser does
(`FILE_MAX_BYTES`, STG-010). The CSV endpoints of section 4 are the ones bound by the host.

`XLS-003` for the workbook, `MAP-001` to `MAP-003` for the column names, and `SPW-001` for the

## 5. Employee lifecycle

Status transitions allowed: hired → probation or confirmed; probation → confirmed or terminated;
confirmed → suspended, resigned, terminated or retired; suspended → confirmed or terminated.
Resigned, terminated and retired are final.

A permanent personnel action (appointment, promotion, demotion, transfer, re-designation, salary
step increment, reassignment) dated **after today, Addis Ababa time**, is held until that date.
Acting assignment, delegation and secondment are never held. `DELETE
/employees/{employee}/personnel-actions/{personnelAction}` cancels a held action
(`personnel_action.create`).
