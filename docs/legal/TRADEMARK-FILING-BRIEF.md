## What to file

| Mark | File? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **ETHR**, word mark | **Yes, first** | Protects the name in any typeface or colour. It is what buyers say and what a competitor would copy |
| ETHR logo (the open lowercase e with the Sunrise dot) | **Yes** | Protects the design. File it in black and white (`docs/brand/ethr-symbol-mono.svg`) to cover every colour, unless counsel advises the colour is part of what makes it distinctive |

## Before filing: the search (register L10)

1. **Search EIPA's register** for ETHR and close variants (ETHER, ETH-R, E-THR, ETHIR) in classes 9, 35 and 42.
2. **Expect a descriptiveness objection.** "ETHR" can be read as short for "Ethiopia HR", a description of the service. Points to make in reply: the mark is a coined spelling, not a dictionary word; it has its own stylised logo; it has been used in trade (keep dated evidence: invoices, the public site, customer contracts).
3. **Look abroad if ETHR may be sold outside Ethiopia.** "Ethr" is used by unrelated technology projects internationally. That does not block an Ethiopian registration, but it limits expansion.
4. **Check company names** at the trade registry for confusingly similar trade names.

## Who files, and when

File in the name of the entity that will own the brand long-term. **If Syntax Technology is converting to a PLC (register L1), either file after conversion or record an assignment to the PLC afterwards.** Keep the filing receipt: the filing date sets priority. Diary the renewal date from the certificate.

## Using the mark

- Use ™ after ETHR until registration, ® only after.
- Use "ETHR" consistently as the product name. Avoid "Ethr", "ethr" or "E-HR" in public material; inconsistency weakens a mark.
- The footer "ETHR. All rights reserved." can add "ETHR is a trademark of Syntax Technology `[confirm legal name]`" once filed.
