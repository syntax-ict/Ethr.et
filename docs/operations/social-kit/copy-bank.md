## 5. Your data, stored in Ethiopia

**Images:** `posts/trust-*.png`, `links/trust-*.png` · **Page:** `/faq`

**LinkedIn**

> Where does your payroll data live?
>
> ETHR keeps your organisation's data on servers in Ethiopia, on Ethio Telecom hosting in Addis Ababa or your own, as Article 22 of the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 requires for personal data collected here.
>
> Bank account, TIN and national ID numbers are encrypted at rest with AES-256. Every query is scoped to one organisation by a guard that fails closed: with no organisation resolved, it returns no rows rather than all of them. Sensitive actions are recorded in an audit trail that cannot be edited or deleted.

> Your organisation stays in control of its data; ETHR supplies the safeguards.

## Stories

`stories/*.png` are cut for Instagram and Facebook Stories and WhatsApp and Telegram Status. Add the platform's link sticker over the lower third, below the drawing and above the gold button, pointing to the pillar's page. Keep stickers out of the top 250 px and bottom 320 px, where the apps draw their own controls.
