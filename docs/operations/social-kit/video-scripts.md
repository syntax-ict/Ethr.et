# Video scripts

Six short videos for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook, each 15 to 25 seconds, vertical 1080 × 1920. Each shows one thing the product does, filmed on a real phone or screen.

## Recording rules

- **Record the demo organisation, on a local install.** Seed it with `php artisan db:seed --class=DemoTenantSeeder` on a machine set up from [`LOCAL_SETUP.md`](../../LOCAL_SETUP.md). Never film a customer's organisation or production data, and never show a sign-in password on screen.
- **Film the real thing.** The offline video works because the phone really is in airplane mode. Do not fake a screen in editing.
- **Captions on every video**, burned in: most people watch without sound. Amharic captions use Noto Sans Ethiopic. On-screen text below comes from the product's dictionary; a new Amharic line needs a native speaker's review.
- **No voice-over is required.** Every script works with on-screen text and music. If you add voice, use the on-screen lines as the script.
- **End card:** the last two seconds show [`stories/trial-en.png`](assets/stories/trial-en.png) or [`stories/trial-am.png`](assets/stories/trial-am.png).
- **Cover image:** the matching `stories/*.png`, or a frame from the video with the headline.
- Use only music the platform licenses for business accounts.
