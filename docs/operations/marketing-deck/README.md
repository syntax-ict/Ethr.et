# Marketing deck: retired

The 13-slide deck that lived here, *ETHR for HR teams*, was retired on 20 November 2026. Its source (`deck.json` and `slides/`) is removed from this folder and remains in the repository's history.

**Do not present it.** It was written on 31 October 2026, and several of its lines are no longer true of the product or were never supported:

| The old deck said | As it stands |
|---|---|
| "One system, two languages" | The product ships in English, Amharic and Afaan Oromoo ([`LOCALIZATION.md`](../../LOCALIZATION.md)) |
| "Works without a network" | A punch made on a phone or a shared kiosk waits on the device and is sent later. Nothing else works offline |
| "Data stored in Ethiopia" | The service runs on hosting in Ethiopia. Off-host backup copies can be kept elsewhere ([`BACKUP-RESTORE.md`](../../deployment/BACKUP-RESTORE.md)) |
| Its brand | The mark and palette changed on 13 November 2026 ([`brand/`](../../brand/)) |

The current sales deck and the rest of the sales material are kept with the company's commercial documents, outside this public repository. The claims any of it may make are still governed here: by the list the server refuses (`api/app/Support/MarketingClaims.php`, described in [`MARKETING-SITE.md`](../MARKETING-SITE.md)) and by the live plan prices at `/api/v1/plans`.

The social media kit beside this folder is still current: [`social-kit/README.md`](../social-kit/README.md).
