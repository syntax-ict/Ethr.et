Six things that go wrong in an HR office

Attendance on paper

Registers are signed late, and overtime is argued about at month end.

Payroll by hand

The tax ladder and pension split live in a spreadsheet, and one wrong cell reaches a payslip.

Leave in a spreadsheet

Only one person can open the file, and the Ethiopian calendar is counted by hand.

Branches lose the network

A regional branch goes offline for an afternoon, and its records for the day go with it.

One language for two teams

Finance works in English, supervisors in Amharic, and one of them is left guessing.

Contracts expire unnoticed

A fixed-term contract ends, nobody is told, and the renewal is signed weeks late.

Ask the room which of these six they lived through this month. Most HR teams will pick three or four. Every slide that follows answers one of them.
