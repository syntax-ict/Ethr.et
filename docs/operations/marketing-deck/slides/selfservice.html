Self-service and approvals

Fewer routine requests reach HR

Check their shift

From any phone, before they leave home.

Request leave

With their own balance in view, counted on the Ethiopian calendar.

Download payslips

Without asking the payroll office for a copy.

Update their details

Changes go to HR for review before they take effect.

Managers decide every request from one approvals inbox. In Amharic or English.

Each card is a question that today walks into the HR office. With ETHR, employees answer it themselves from a phone, and managers handle what is left from one inbox.
