What ETHR does for attendance

Phone, QR code, kiosk and web

Choose how each site checks in. A shared kiosk serves staff who do not carry a smartphone.

Location check

Set an area around each workplace, and a phone check-in from outside it can be refused.

Works offline

Punches are stored on the device and sync when the connection returns.

Shifts and overtime

Rotating and night shifts, grace periods and overtime rules per department.

Corrections on record
