Your data, stored in Ethiopia

ETHR keeps your organisation's data on servers in Ethiopia, on Ethio Telecom hosting or your own, as the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 requires. Your organisation stays in control of its data; ETHR supplies the safeguards below.

  • Data residency

    Stored on servers in Ethiopia, as Article 22 of Proclamation 1321/2024 requires for personal data collected here.

  • Encryption at rest

    Bank account, TIN and national ID numbers are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

  • Tenant isolation

    Every query is scoped to one organization by a guard that fails closed: with no organization resolved, it returns no rows rather than all of them.

  • Audit trail

    Sensitive actions are recorded in an audit trail that cannot be edited or deleted.
